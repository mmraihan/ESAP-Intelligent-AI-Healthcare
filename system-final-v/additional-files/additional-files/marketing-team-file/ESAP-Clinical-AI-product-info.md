## Short Product Description

ESAP Clinical AI is a **cloud-based (SaaS) healthcare platform** that brings AI insights, hospital teams, patients and other hospitals together in one place. Hospitals subscribe and go live without installing anything. Each hospital gets its own secure, private space on the platform.

**At admission, AI gives an early picture of the patient.** Staff enter the information available when a patient arrives, such as age, vital signs, first blood tests, past conditions, medicines and the admission note. Within seconds, ESAP gives five early insights:

1. **Possible conditions:** a ranked list of conditions the patient may have
2. **Risk of death** during the hospital stay
3. **Chance of needing intensive care (ICU)**
4. **Chance of returning to hospital** within 30 days
5. **Expected length of stay** in hospital

Each insight comes with a simple **"Why?"** explanation, so staff can check the reasoning. The product says openly when an insight is less reliable, and it is checked regularly for fairness across patient groups.

**It is also useful before a patient is admitted.** Patients can create their own account, choose a hospital and ask to connect with it. They can describe their symptoms and fill in their details before a visit, and share them with the care team. A clinician then accepts the request and turns it into an admission. Individual doctors can also sign up on their own, without a hospital, to try the insights.

**Hospitals can collaborate with each other.** A hospital can request a patient's records from another hospital. The receiving hospital reviews and approves the request before anything is shared, and every exchange is based on the patient's consent and recorded.

**One platform for everyone involved in care:**
- **Doctors and nurses** get a patient worklist, AI insights, shift handover and discharge planning.
- **Hospital administrators** manage staff, settings, privacy, fairness and billing.
- **Patients and families** see their results in plain language, in English or Arabic, and decide who can see their records.
- **The ESAP team** onboards and supports many hospitals from one central console.

The product **supports doctors' decisions; it does not replace them.** A clinician always makes the final call.

## Key Features

### For doctors and nurses
- **Five AI insights at admission**, each with a clear "Why?" explanation
- **Honest confidence labels:** the product says openly when an insight is less reliable
- **Patient worklist**, sortable by ward, filterable to "my patients" and searchable
- **Quick triage and new admissions:** add a new patient and get insights straight away
- **Admission form** with ready-made sample patients, condition and medicine search, and save-as-draft
- **Chat assistant:** describe a patient in plain words and the form fills itself in
- **Ask about a patient:** ask questions about a patient's record and insights
- **Review and sign-off:** doctors confirm they have reviewed a result, and senior doctors countersign trainees' work
- **Patient timeline and history:** what was known and when, plus every past assessment
- **Vital signs** recording at the bedside
- **Tasks and escalation:** to-do lists and quick escalation of a deteriorating patient
- **Shift handover** notes between teams
- **Discharge planning:** patients ranked by their chance of returning to hospital
- **Nurse ward roster** and staff overview
- **Release results to the patient** once a clinician has reviewed them
- **Patient submissions:** see the symptoms and details a patient shared before their visit
- **Connection requests:** accept patients who asked to join the hospital
- **Request records from another hospital**, directly from the patient's page

### For hospital administrators
- **Hospital profile and branding**, plus hospital-wide policies
- **Facilities and wards** setup
- **Staff management:** invite staff, assign roles, control access, reset passwords and deactivate accounts
- **15 job roles**, each seeing only what that job needs
- **Hospital-to-hospital record exchange:** approve or reject record requests
- **Connections** to the hospital's existing systems, including staff single sign-on
- **Audit log:** a record of who did what, and when
- **Fairness checks:** results reviewed across patient groups
- **AI insight display settings:** choose how insights are shown to staff
- **Usage reports and billing**, plus data exports, which are always recorded

### For patients and families
- **Self sign-up and choosing a hospital:** patients can join before being admitted
- **Pre-visit check-in:** describe symptoms and share details ahead of a visit
- **Plain-language results**, with the option to ask a question or ask a nurse to explain
- **Medical records**, with download, requests for corrections, and requests for records from other hospitals
- **Care team:** see who is caring for you and message them
- **Sharing and consent:** choose who can see your records and review who accessed them
- **Family and caregiver access:** invite a family member to help manage care
- **English and Arabic** language support
- **Account settings**, including password change and account closure

### For the ESAP platform team (SaaS management)
- **All hospitals in one view:** health, usage and status of every customer hospital
- **Onboarding new hospitals** in a few steps
- **Subscription plans** management
- **Gradual roll-out** of new AI model versions to hospitals
- **Incident tracking and status updates**
- **Emergency access ("break-glass")** with a second person's approval, always recorded

### Across the whole platform
- **Privacy and security:** each person sees only what their role allows, with two-step login and a full activity record
- **Public website:** product overview, evidence, security and pricing pages
- **Light and dark mode**, and phone-friendly screens
