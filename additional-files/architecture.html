MIMIC-IV · concurrent comorbidity inference

An anytime differential engine.
It gets sharper with every data point.

Give it whatever the patient brings — symptoms alone, or symptoms plus history, meds, vitals, labs. It returns a ranked, calibrated differential of likely conditions, the single most useful next test, and a plain-language reason for every call. Add the test result; the differential re-ranks.

01

Dataflow — input to output

One leakage firewall, three specialist models, one fused answer. Each model is auditable on its own; the fusion layer trusts whichever has signal for this patient.

A · always on · CPU

Tabular floor

Gradient boosting over structured features. The calibration & fairness reference, and the cold-start fallback.

reads: demographics · vitals · prior-dx · home-meds · labs · Charlson/Elixhauser

B · biggest lift · GPU

Clinical text

Clinical-Longformer over arrival-only note sections. Carries patients with no prior record.

reads: chief complaint · HPI · PMH · meds-on-admission

C · repeat patients · GPU

Coded sequence

Med-BERT-style transformer over the patient's prior-admission timeline (CCSR-tokenized).

reads: prior diagnoses · procedures · meds · labs, mapped through CCSR

Fusion · calibrated meta-learnerroutes by available data

Stacks the three towers' predictions plus flags for what each patient actually has. Text wins when there's a note; the sequence model wins for repeat patients; the tabular floor always votes.

if has_note → weight B · if has_history → weight C · always → A · then isotonic calibration
